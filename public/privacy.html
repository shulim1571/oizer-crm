FundBase Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

FundBase by Trubase ("FundBase") is a donation and campaign management service operated by Shulim Mueller DBA Trubase ("Trubase", "we", "us"). Website: trubase.org. Contact: smueller@trubase.org.

This policy explains what information FundBase handles, why, and the choices you have. FundBase is used by nonprofit organizations ("organizations", our customers) and their staff. Each organization controls the information it puts into FundBase; we process it on the organization's behalf.

1. Information we handle

Staff accounts. Name, email address, phone number (if added for notifications or text lookups), role, password (stored hashed by our authentication provider), two-step verification settings, and sign-in records.

Sign in with Google. If a staff member chooses to sign in with Google, Google shares their name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID with FundBase. We use them only to identify the staff member and sign them in to their organization's account. We do not request access to Gmail, contacts, calendar, files or any other Google data, and we do not use information from Google for advertising, sell it, or use it to train AI models.

Organization records entered by staff. Campaigns, donations, pledges, payouts, donor names and contact details, receipts and notes, as the organization records them.

Donations made on a FundBase payment page. Name, contact details, amount and the details needed to process the payment. Card and bank account numbers are sent to the payment processor (Sola Payments) and are not stored by FundBase, except a processor token and the last four digits where a donor asks to save a card for recurring gifts.

Bank account data through Plaid. If an organization's administrator chooses to connect the organization's own bank account, FundBase uses Plaid Inc. ("Plaid") to retrieve that account's transactions so staff can reconcile their records. You sign in to your bank through Plaid's own screen; FundBase never sees or stores your bank username or password. We receive and store only, for the one account you select: posted transactions (date, amount, description or merchant name, check number and Plaid's transaction ID), the account's name and last four digits, the institution name, and an access token that lets us request new transactions. We do not request or store account or routing numbers, balances, or account-owner identity information.

Technical information. IP addresses and request logs kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting.

2. How we use information

  • To provide FundBase to organizations: record and report donations, reconcile bank accounts, send receipts and notifications the organization sets up, and keep accounts secure.
  • Bank data from Plaid is used only to show and match the organization's own bank transactions in its reconciliation screen. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or marketing, use it for credit or lending decisions, or use it to train AI models.
  • An optional in-app help assistant answers questions about how to use FundBase using Anthropic's Claude service. It receives the question and a fixed guide to the app; it does not receive donor, donation or bank records.

3. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that run FundBase for us, under their terms, and only as needed:

Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services, USA)Database, sign-in, server functions, backups
CloudflareWebsite hosting, security and bot protection
PlaidBank connection and transactions (only if an organization connects a bank account)
Sola Payments (Cardknox)Card and bank-transfer donation processing
Twilio and SendGridText messages, phone lookups and email
AnthropicOptional help assistant (no records are sent)
GitHub, GoogleSource code hosting; website fonts and domain name service; Sign in with Google (if a staff member chooses it)

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the security of FundBase and its users.

Plaid's handling of data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy at https://plaid.com/legal/.

4. Security

Information is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data is stored with our database provider's encryption at rest, and Plaid access tokens are additionally encrypted. Each organization's data is separated from other organizations' at the database level. Bank and reconciliation data is available only to the organization's administrators after two-step verification. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected organizations of a security incident as described in our incident response plan and as the law requires.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Bank connections can be disconnected at any time by the organization's administrator; disconnecting revokes our access at Plaid and deletes the stored access token immediately.
  • Bank transactions already brought in are kept as part of the organization's reconciliation records while the organization uses FundBase, and are deleted on the organization's request or within 30 days after the organization leaves FundBase.
  • Backups are kept for 7 days and then deleted.
  • Other records are kept while the organization uses FundBase and deleted on its request or within 30 days after it leaves, unless we must keep them longer by law.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Staff can update their own account details and turn two-step verification on or off under Account security.
  • Donors and other individuals whose information an organization recorded should contact that organization first; we will help the organization respond.
  • To ask about, correct or delete information, contact smueller@trubase.org. We respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights (for example under California law); we honor them as required.

7. Children

FundBase is for organizations and their staff, not for children under 13, and is not directed to them.

8. Changes

We will post changes to this policy with a new date, and tell organizations about material changes.
